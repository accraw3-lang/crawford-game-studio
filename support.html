MISFIRE Support

Having trouble with MISFIRE? We can help.

Purchase or restore issues

MISFIRE uses a one-time non-consumable purchase to unlock the full game.

If you previously purchased the unlock, use Restore Purchase on the Moon unlock screen.

Purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

Gameplay issues

If MISFIRE is crashing, behaving unexpectedly, or not saving progress correctly, please report the problem through our support tracker.

Report a problem

For bugs, purchase issues, or feedback, open a support issue on our public GitHub support tracker.

Open a Support Issue

Include this information

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your MISFIRE app version
  • A short description of what happened
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the issue
