Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

MISFIRE

MISFIRE does not require an account and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

Game progress, including high scores and unlock status, is stored locally on your device.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Crawford Game Studio does not receive or store your payment card information.

Data collection

Crawford Game Studio does not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information from MISFIRE users.

Support requests

If you contact us through the Crawford Game Studio support tracker, GitHub may process information you choose to provide under GitHub's own privacy practices.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information in public support requests.

Children

MISFIRE does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if MISFIRE's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the Crawford Game Studio support tracker.
